Research, methods & sources

The CSV is a list of SNP genotypes. Each calculator matches autosomal rsIDs to its own published allele-frequency panel and estimates nonnegative proportions summing to 100% by maximizing the diploid binomial likelihood. K36 uses 165,688 reference SNPs; K12b uses 166,770. They are fitted independently from your DNA.

We support quoted MyHeritage CSV and tab-separated files with either a combined genotype or two allele columns. Missing calls, indels, sex chromosomes and conflicting duplicate rsIDs are excluded. Matching uses rsID rather than genomic position; unambiguous strand complements are aligned to the model alleles. Coverage is reported separately for each model. At least 10,000 matched SNPs are required here; below 40,000, results are marked as low coverage.

G25 is estimated from the full-precision K36 fractions using an ordinary least squares regression fitted to 1,119 matching modern samples from 137 populations. The output is already scaled. A reproducible 80/20 individual holdout (223 test samples) gave a median Euclidean error of 0.02934 and a 95th percentile of 0.06881 in scaled coordinates, before the ×100 distance display used elsewhere in this calculator. These are aggregate validation errors, not a personal confidence interval. Populations can occur in both splits; this does not measure performance on unseen populations, and error from raw-DNA fitting adds further uncertainty.

Component names describe similarity to model clusters; they do not directly measure recent ancestors. Results can differ from GEDmatch because SNP coverage, allele handling and numerical fitting differ.
