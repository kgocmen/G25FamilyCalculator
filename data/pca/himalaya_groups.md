# Himalaya — PCA 20

The Himalaya panel brings together 59 existing G25 population references from
South Asia, East Asia, and Southeast Asia. It uses 56 Main references and three
additional Deep references, with seven subgroups:

| Subgroup | Populations represented | Main | Total including Deep |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Tibet and Sherpa | Tibetan regional profiles and Sherpa | 17 | 17 |
| Nepal Tibeto-Burman | Gurung, Rai, Magar, and Tamang | 6 | 6 |
| Newar | Newar and the Magar Newar-profile reference | 2 | 2 |
| Nepal Indo-Aryan | Khas and Madhesi Brahmin references | 5 | 5 |
| Western Himalaya | Kashmir, Himachal, Garhwal, Kumaon, Balti, Brokpa, and Burusho profiles | 17 | 19 |
| Himalayan Foothills | Kusunda, Tharu, Rajbanshi, and Tibetan Muslim Srinagar | 4 | 4 |
| Eastern Himalaya | Gyalrong, Pumi, Naxi, Baima, and Sichuan Garze Qiang profiles | 5 | 6 |

This is a regional reference panel spanning substantial genetic variation, not
a single ancestry component or language family. Placement follows the named
regional populations rather than a largest-Neolithic-proxy rule. The distinct
subgroups preserve that variation for the calculator's fitting and Deep analysis.

The references move out of their previous panels. Source coordinates and
Main / Deep placement are preserved except for Pumi, which is promoted to Main
to represent the eastern subgroup: otherwise its main fit selects only Tibet
and Sherpa and excludes its own Deep reference. Punjab and North India stays in South
Asia, and Iranic remains unchanged. The Guizhou Qiang reference stays in East
Asia's Southern China subgroup; Khasi and Manipuri Brahmin stay in Southeast
Asia's Indo-Burmese subgroup.
